MADE FOR ANOTHER CHAPTER

Useful parts.
Thoughtful exchanges.

ReLoop is an exchange for physical electronic components. A donor and a repairer agree on a match and complete the handover through their local repair community.

01

List a component

Describe one actual part. Add its manufacturer code, supported models, condition and general pickup area.

02

Check the fit

Search by code or device model. Verify connector type and manufacturer specifications. Similar model names alone cannot establish compatibility.

03

Reserve your match

Sign in, confirm compatibility and submit a repair note. The exchange accepts a single active reservation for each part.

04

Record the collection

Arrange pickup through your repair club. The donor confirms physical handover. Either person can cancel an active request beforehand.

What this version includes

Accounts, listings, search filters, reservations, cancellation, collection history and administrator review of reports. The same business rules power the web interface and REST API.

Pickup coordination happens outside the app. This version does not include chat, payments, delivery, automatic compatibility certification or verified testing of components.

Example listings labelled “FICTIONAL DEMO LISTING” use invented codes and devices. They are demonstration records rather than real inventory.
